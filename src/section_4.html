02

How long do I have access to this course?

You get 4 months of full access to all course modules, practical project files, and content updates during your access period, so you can learn at your own pace.

03

Do I need any prior editing experience?

No prior experience is needed. The course begins with editing fundamentals and guides you step-by-step to advanced cinematic techniques.

04

Can I learn CapCut on Windows or Mac?

Yes! The masterclass covers CapCut Desktop (Windows/Mac) with complete step-by-step practical workflows.

05

Is the free CapCut Pro account included?

Yes, upon enrolling in the Masterclass, you will receive 01 months of full CapCut Pro access included with your student account.
